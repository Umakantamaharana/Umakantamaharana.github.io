<div class="bio-content">
  <p class="lead">
    Hi! I am <strong>Umakanta Maharana</strong>, a researcher passionate about <strong>Large Language Models (LLMs)</strong>, <strong>AI Safety & Alignment</strong>, and <strong>Reliable Machine Learning</strong>.
  </p>
  <p>
    My work focuses on evaluating and enhancing the practical reliability of frontier AI systems. I explore questions such as whether models faithfully follow complex organizational access policies (e.g., our <strong><a href="/projects/orgaccess/">OrgAccess</a></strong> benchmark, accepted at <strong>EMNLP 2025</strong>), uncovering misalignment between clinical accuracy and underlying diagnostic reasoning in healthcare, and developing adaptive multi-agent frameworks for personalized education.
  </p>
</div>
